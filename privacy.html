Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Taz (the “Application”), a Discord bot/application, processes information when you interact with it. The developer of Taz is responsible for the Application’s own processing. Discord separately processes information under Discord’s Privacy Policy.

1. Information the Application may process

When you use a bot command or interact with Taz, Discord may make the information needed to handle that interaction available to the Application. Depending on the feature used, this may include:

  • Your Discord user ID and username/display name;
  • The server (guild) ID and channel ID where the interaction occurs;
  • The command, options, or text you intentionally submit to the bot;
  • Interaction metadata, such as the time of an interaction and the response status.

The Application should only access information needed to provide the feature you request. It is not intended to collect passwords, payment-card details, or sensitive personal information.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to receive, understand, and respond to bot commands; provide the requested features; maintain the security and reliability of the Application; and respond to support or privacy requests.

3. Storage and retention

Template assumption — confirm before publishing: This policy currently assumes that Taz processes command and interaction data temporarily to respond and does not intentionally keep a persistent database or retain message/command contents after the interaction is completed. We do not sell personal information. Information is not shared with advertisers. If additional providers, analytics, databases, or logging services are used, they must be identified here before those services process user data.
